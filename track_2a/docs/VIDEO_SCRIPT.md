# Demo script

The reviewed demo should stay below two minutes. It uses the actual Zusage interface with fictional data and standard US English neural narration. Captions are supplied separately and burned into the preview. Avoid long pauses.

1. First interviews deserve practice: show the new landing page.
2. Choose an occupation, interviewer, mode and one of five interview languages.
3. Lea gives a short answer, rates it and receives evidence-based feedback.
4. She retries with a real trial-day example and inspects the changed feedback.
5. Show the final report and next practice goals.
6. Connect practice to an application through the tracker.
7. Show the teacher classroom view and explain student consent.
8. Close with Apertus 1.5 8B, measured calls per answer and https://zusage.web.app.

Human approval of the actual voiceover and video is required before publishing the final demo or submitting. Never invent a recording of an interaction that did not occur; the edited walkthrough uses captured app states.
