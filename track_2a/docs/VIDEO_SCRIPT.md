# Zusage demo narration

Standard US English synthetic narration. Edited walkthrough of the tested live app with fictional learner data.

Your first interview should not be your first chance to practise. Meet Zusage, an interview coach for Swiss apprenticeships. It helps young people find their own words before the real conversation.

Choose an apprenticeship, an interviewer, and a language. German, French, Italian, English, or Swiss German. Get coaching after each answer, or rehearse with feedback saved for the end.

Lea starts with a short answer. She reflects before seeing feedback. The coach quotes her actual words and gives one next step: describe a real moment when she helped someone.

She tries again with a story from her trial apprenticeship. The feedback updates, and the score comparison shows what changed. Her own experience stays at the centre of the answer.

The final report shows strengths, six criteria and practical goals. Weak answers return as short, spaced drills. The dashboard connects practice to the next real interview.

Track companies and interview dates. Use a job advert to shape the rehearsal, so practice stays relevant to the company the learner wants to join.

Create editable cover letters and prep packs from real stories. Review every draft before sending. Import existing applications through CSV, and manage follow-up reminders in the same workspace.

Teachers see class progress. Answer text is shared only with student consent. Each demo visitor gets a private workspace, and schools can deploy the Docker app locally.

Zusage uses Apertus one point five, eight billion parameters. Live tests averaged about one point one model calls per answer. Try Zusage dot web dot app. Practise, reflect, try again.
